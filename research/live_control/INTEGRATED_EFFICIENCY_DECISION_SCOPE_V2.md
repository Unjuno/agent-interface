# Integrated-efficiency decision scope v2 — prospective only

This clarification is prospective for any future #57 allocation. It does not
modify, rerun or reinterpret integrated-efficiency-live-01. That frozen
allocation remains RETAIN for its declared finite schedule, exactly as its
report records.

## Two distinct decisions

1. **Finite-allocation disposition.** Apply the frozen per-allocation rule to
   the named tasks and schedule. RETAIN means only that the finite allocation
   met its declared correctness, accounting, comparability and route thresholds.
   HOLD means evidence/accounting/comparability is incomplete, or an
   integration discovery invalidates that allocation. REJECT means a
   verified correctness/safety failure or a frozen performance threshold fails.
   Unknown safety/effect/accounting is not a pass. Do not change thresholds
   after assignment or rerun a failed allocation under the same freeze.
2. **Generalization / external claim scope.** A finite RETAIN alone never
   establishes a population success rate, general route advantage, human-tempo
   gain, product completion, or DOOM capability. Mark those claims
   HOLD_NOT_ESTABLISHED until a separate prospective design freezes the
   population, replication/assignment and uncertainty method, missing-outcome
   treatment and meaningful-gain decision rule. No arbitrary sample minimum or
   confidence threshold is introduced by this clarification.

Report both outputs side by side. In the prior one-sequence-per-arm allocation,
RETAIN and HOLD_NOT_ESTABLISHED can both be true without contradiction:
the first is bounded to the frozen six-task schedule; the second is the
unresolved wider claim. This removes the ambiguous phrase “the single
allocation is insufficient for the rule” from prospective use. If evidence is
insufficient for a specified finite gate, apply that gate's HOLD condition;
if the finite gate is met but generalization was not designed, hold only the
generalized claim.

The paired all-attempt design refinement on #57 still applies to any future
comparison: preserve assigned pair IDs, independently reset both copies, keep
FAIL/UNKNOWN rows and all attempts in the denominator, report paired and
unpaired summaries, and keep safety as a hard gate.

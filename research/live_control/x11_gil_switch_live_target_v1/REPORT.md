## Why the two 1 ms misses matter

A non-gating posthoc alignment analysis uses only retained timestamps. The two 1 ms misses have no acquisition start during the valid cue interval. Their acquisition-start gaps spanning the cue midpoint are `6.145681` ms and `7.223926` ms, longer than the ~5.09 ms cue. All eight 1 ms detections have at least one acquisition start inside the cue. The single 5 ms detection is likewise the only 5 ms case with an acquisition start inside the cue.

This supports a narrower explanation: residual misses are consistent with scheduler/observer gaps that fully bridge the cue. It does not prove which scheduler mechanism caused each gap.

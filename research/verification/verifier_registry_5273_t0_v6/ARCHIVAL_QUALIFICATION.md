The raw envelope contains no run timestamp, source/head identity, invocation ID, elapsed-time or process receipt. 4. **No v6 audit-result or test log:** the twelve published files contain the auditor source and tests, but no separately retained audit-output JSON, stdout/stderr, exit-code or test-run receipt. The historical 21/21 and raw-auditor PASS reports cannot be independently established from such missing receipts. They remain reported outcomes, not a new reproduction.
5. **Construction failures are narrative evidence:** the v6 report retains initial missing-behavior failures (coverage, cold budget, deadline precedence, prohibited side effect, explicit unavailable state), duplicate-descriptor ambiguity and missing output-category metadata. It publishes no separate v6 failing-run logs. Preserve that distinction rather than inventing failure receipts.
6. **Limited audit contract:** the retained auditor reads the frozen corpus/raw and imports a separate `oracle.py`, not `candidate.py`; it does not read or enforce `FREEZE.json`'s source hashes. Its descriptor-binding branch indexes the first check/assignment/descriptor and the published eight raw cases are single-check. Neither those cases nor separate construction tests establish a general multi-check scheduler or universal independent-auditor soundness. The archived source is not hardened or repaired here.

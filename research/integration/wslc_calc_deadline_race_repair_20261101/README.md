# WSLc Calc: deadline race in research recovery harness

Three preregistered isolated Calc allocations investigate a two-second fresh-owner reobservation bound after a compiled entry yields. All use a fixed 731/864 fixture, two `gpt-6.1-sol` low-effort model calls, a test-controlled cell overlay and separate original/fresh owners. The original entry executes once and its owner releases input. The candidate runtime is a retained snapshot, not asserted identical to current main.

| Allocation | Controlled condition | Observed outcome |
|---|---|---|
| eg01 | Obstruction held through expiry, cleared only after the loop recognized expiry; then observe for ≥500 ms without input | 16 blocked observations, four later matching observations, no fresh mint or program; saved file remained blank. The last blocked capture began 12.578 ms after the nominal deadline and expiry was recognized 44.722 ms late. |
| eh01 | Overlay cleared 50.363 ms after deadline but **before the next observation**, preserving the original success-before-expiry check | The matching capture began 152.625 ms after deadline; the harness nevertheless minted and dispatched Save 218.657 ms after deadline. The saved FODS contained 731/864. This is a **failure of the research harness's intended deadline**. |
| ei01 | Same staged race, but check monotonic expiry immediately after observation and before accepting the cue | The matching capture began 154.208 ms after deadline; no fresh mint or program was issued and the saved FODS remained blank. The runner exited with the expected bounded refusal. |

All original owner releases and final X key/button states were verified neutral. The positive task effect in eh01 does not make the late dispatch correct. The fix is in this retained research candidate; it has **not** been integrated into or established as a defect in the production controller. The check ordering change prevents the demonstrated path in one successor run but is not a hard real-time guarantee or a general asynchronous cancellation proof. The harness deliberately controls the overlay and injects timing to expose the boundary; no arbitrary edits, partial-keystroke recovery, general reliability, comparative efficiency or MAP01 completion are established. ef01's earlier timely-clear success remains separate.

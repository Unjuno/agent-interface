This single preregistered control follows dh01 (`wslc_map01_natural_cancel_timing_20261101`) with the same seed, image and guarded 500 ms fire path, enabling a baseline route in the candidate runner that omits the patch watcher. The source snapshot is not claimed to equal current main. Model calls and target selection were independent: dh01 chose (656,526), di01 chose (636,526). Hence this is a directional control, not a matched or randomized causal comparison.

The di01 model chose fire, all recorded guards passed, the 500 ms wait completed in 500.095627 ms and the owner verified no keys or buttons down. Ammo changed 50 to 48, hits 0 to 1, and damage 0 to 15; kills stayed 0. In dh01 the watcher requested cancellation on natural image change, the action's partial effect was unknown, ammo changed 50 to 49, and counters showed the same one hit and 15 damage. One observation per arm cannot establish the cause of the differing shot count, show that the natural change was dangerous or harmless, estimate error rates, or prove MAP01 completion. A stronger follow-up needs matched scripted action and target, replicated seeds and timing, and event labels independent of the patch detector.

## H/T/D/C/U reconciliation

### T

The retained comparison contains three arms (plain, ephemeral, persistent), six tasks per arm, independent cold schema preflight, repeated A use, a layout change at task 4, bounded repair, and repeated B use. Plain and persistent task-6 input tokens are 63,128 and 26,563; planner generations are 7 and 3; model-visible image calls are 6 and 2; elapsed times are 56.412 s and 44.131 s. Every arm scored 6/6 exact submissions. Persistent task 4 rejected the old reference as `MISSING` with zero pointer admissions, then repaired from the current layout.

The retained cumulative reconstruction reports break-even at task 2 versus plain and task 1 versus ephemeral. The post-repair reconstruction reports persistent task 4–6 input tokens 9,299 versus ephemeral 27,906, with +3 local observations and +10 durable calls; these units are not converted into token or cost equivalents.

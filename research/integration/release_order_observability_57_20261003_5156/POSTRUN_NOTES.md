modified during publication work. `readback_checks.py`, its result/streams/receipt and
this documentation were added after execution; they are not prospectively frozen
scientific inputs and do not count as another primary run.

and exit 1. The subsequent search mistakenly listed the full recursive research tree

reported as zero original captures checked. Published source/data can still be checked
against their Git/freeze/manifest pins without private host paths.

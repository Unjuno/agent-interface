# Explicit commit and late acknowledgement: r523

The preregistered four-arm synthetic trial ended **ERROR** (exit 1). In the valid arm, parent verified the prepared-record digest, sent explicit COMMIT, received its acknowledgement, and guardian plus independent pidfds observed owner/supervisor exit. Mismatched owner PID was rejected with a guardian no-signal receipt. A simulated pre-write stall produced parent timeout with no COMMIT and no prepared record or receipt at observation. These three arms' recorded checks were true; they are not a general reliability result.

In the late-ack arm, the parent recorded `timeout` and `commit_sent=false`, and the prepared record existed. The expected guardian receipt did not appear within eight seconds. The fixture then reported ERROR and performed cleanup. Guardian stderr, exception and per-stage timing were not captured, so the raw cannot establish whether acknowledgement was sent, whether the guardian observed EOF or a reset, or whether it sent any signal. Cleanup pidfd exit observations include observer-issued SIGKILL and are not independent proof of guardian behavior.

One plausible explanation is a Unix stream reset when the parent closes with unread acknowledgement bytes. A separate local socketpair probe produced `ConnectionResetError` on that pattern, but it is not direct evidence of what occurred in this trial. A fresh diagnostic trial needs guardian stderr and stage/errno observations, bounded collection and cleanup-before-signal liveness evidence; no explicit COMMIT must mean no signal, with any lost final commit acknowledgement remaining UNKNOWN.

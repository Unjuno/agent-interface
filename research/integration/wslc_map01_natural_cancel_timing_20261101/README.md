# WSLc MAP01: natural patch change and cancellation timing, dh01

A single model-selected fire action (capped to 500 ms) ran on a private Xvfb display with no injected overlay. The same frozen candidate and seed were used in the earlier dg01 artificial-overlay positive control; model calls were separate and outputs were not matched. Conditions were recorded before launch.

The sampled 8x8 patch changed naturally: mean absolute RGB difference from the admitted reference rose from 16.734375 to 45.572917 (threshold >32). The monitor requested cancellation, and the action owner independently verified neutral keys and buttons 9.375950 ms after callback start; the callback took 0.011770 ms including result copying. The monitor acknowledgement itself does not verify release. The interrupted operation remains partial-effect unknown. Simulator counters show ammo 50 to 49, hits 0 to 1 and damage 0 to 15; kills remained 0. These counters establish observed task-relevant effect, not complete MAP01 or a causal explanation for the patch change. The one-run time is not a latency bound; sampled pixel change is not semantic danger detection, and callbacks can still block. No claims of a controlled speed comparison or general reliability follow from these two separate model runs.

`public.zip` preserves the frozen candidate, conditions, raw images and observations, model result metadata, launcher output, hashes for private files, and a member manifest.

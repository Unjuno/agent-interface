# Correction: delayed-XTEST fixture multiplicity

The r137, r141, r142, r143, r144, r146 and r147 records in these two directories used `_xvfb_blocking_owner_factory` before the idempotence fix in [#10103](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/pull/10103). Each guarded operation could call the fixture's wrapped `get`, installing another sync and XTEST wrapper. A focused regression saw one intended `a` press cause four delayed `b` injections after four `get` calls; the fixture used the same marker filename for each injection. A separate instrumented child captured four nested fixture sync wrappers. Thus these records do not establish exactly one queued delayed request, nor that the recorded marker identifies the first request. Their fixed horizon is measured from a marker that may have been overwritten; it is not a general queue-drain fence or evidence of production latency.

The preserved keymap readings, target event traces, operation receipts, forced interleavings and original source hashes are still observations under their documented fixture and timing. In particular, the r147 new-owner hold changing from down to up after the old normal cancellation remains an observed same-display interference in that controlled trial; it does not establish spontaneous frequency. Earlier per-arm RESULTS, raw files and manifests remain unchanged as historical records and should be read with this correction.

After the fixture fix, a separate local WSLc non-retired control r162 logged exactly one injection; its target saw `b` around eight seconds and the original sync returned. A separate recovery trial r163 logged one injection and saw no `b` on a replacement target through 8.5 seconds from flush. These are single allocations with different retirement conditions; their conditions and raw data are preserved in [corrected fixture evidence](corrected_fixture/README.md). A client flush does not prove server acceptance in the recovery trial. Neither result establishes old-task success, MCP integration, physical-desktop recovery, MAP01, or a general fence.

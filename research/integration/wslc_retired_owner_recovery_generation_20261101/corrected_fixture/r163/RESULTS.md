# Corrected-fixture private recovery and delayed-request horizon

One new WSLc/private-Xvfb allocation uses the same selected runtime and idempotent, injection-logging fixture as r162's non-retired positive control. With a real guarded owner holding `a`, the fixture log records exactly one delayed `b` injection and a blocked X sync. The actual draft `PrivateDisplayRecovery` cancels the owner and retires its owned Xvfb, leaving old operation and cleanup UNKNOWN and replay disallowed. The old synthetic application is destroyed with that display.

A fresh owned Xvfb reused `:0`. A bounded old-authority connection probe failed with `DisplayConnectionError`; the new-authority control connected. The probe did not preserve stderr and therefore does not prove the precise rejection reason. A new guarded owner completed an `a` chord on a fresh target and confirmed its own release. At 8.503 seconds after the old client's delayed-request flush, the new target recorded only `a` press/release and independent new keymap showed `a` and `b` up. The new and old displays were retired; credentials remained in container `/tmp`.

In the separate earlier r162 non-retired positive allocation the corrected fixture also logged exactly one injection and the old target observed a `b` press at roughly eight seconds; the original sync and action then completed. The allocations are fixed-order, single attempts and differ in retirement, display replacement, and target; the positive control does not prove r163's old X server accepted the flushed request. This supports a bounded observation, not a general queue fence, automatic MCP integration, transparent old-task recovery, physical input release, MAP01 or reliability claim. Raw events, conditions and source hashes are retained.

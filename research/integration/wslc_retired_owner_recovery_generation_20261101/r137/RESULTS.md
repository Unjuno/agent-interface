# Explicit retired-owner cleanup on a held key

Two serial, single-attempt private WSLc/Xvfb allocations use the same local r132 source, pinned image and manifest. See the two `conditions.json` files, `manifest.json`, private commands and complete outputs. The source is based on draft #10094 and is not merged. The comparison deliberately pauses the parent after a real child cancellation acknowledgement, retires the old child while `a` is held, and resumes the candidate. This is an injected race, not a normal end-to-end cancellation run.

Both arms independently observed `a` down before and after cancellation, the real child returned `cancel_requested`, and the candidate returned `cancel_unconfirmed` / `UNKNOWN` without calling the stubbed cleanup. In the control, the fixture manually released input; keys remained up past the delayed-b horizon and a fresh owner typed a second `a`, producing target value `aa` with verified release.

In the treatment, the test explicitly called the existing private `_cleanup_after_owner_retirement` helper with the real retirement receipt while the display remained isolated and before a new owner existed. The helper read the retired child's shared obligations (`a`, keycode 38), but the PID-qualified disconnect supervision returned `unknown: supervision_timeout` after 151.26 ms; its worker was retired. Consequently release was `not_attempted: owner_disconnect_not_confirmed`, and independent keymap still showed `a` down. The treatment failed its prespecified criteria and stopped before the delayed horizon or a fresh task. The fixture then destroyed the private Xvfb; this is not an observed runtime release. The cause of the disconnect timeout is not established; one result does not show whether it is deterministic or whether disconnect itself failed.

This is evidence that the attempted explicit existing helper did not recover this instance, not proof that the helper cannot work. Direct private invocation is not an integrated recovery path; old close remained UNKNOWN. No physical desktop, MAP01, general latency, or repeatability claim follows. Both containers exited and `wslc list` was empty after the runs.

## C — alternatives/confounders
The rare C failure may be seed-specific optimizer behavior, under-representation in 16 examples, or a generator corner rather than support count. Increasing support changes the empirical sample but preserves its prefix, so it may improve, leave unchanged, or harm held-out accuracy. Ten paired seeds still have limited power for a rare tail event. Exact JSON reload and graph receipts do not prove natural skill transfer; SHA-256 is integrity, not authenticity.

## U — scope

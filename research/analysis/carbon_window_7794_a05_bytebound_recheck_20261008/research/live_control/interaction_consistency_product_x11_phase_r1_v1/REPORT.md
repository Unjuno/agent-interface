## Safety discriminators

The shared-global family demonstrates why a surface-only rule is insufficient. Both surfaces are distinct, but their delayed effects read one root-global slot. The product contract sees the declared conflict/noncommutativity and serializes, preserving A/B. The surface-only comparator overlaps the same phases and deterministically contaminates A to B in 3/3 cases.

The stale-readset family demonstrates the currentness gate independently of resource conflict. B is prepared at local generation0, the fixture advances B to generation1 before B commit, and the product path emits **zero B task input** while the receipt is stale. One explicit refresh binds generation1, after which serial execution completes the exact B effect.

## Scope

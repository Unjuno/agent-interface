## H / T / D / C / U

- **H:** A deterministic source-bound synthesis gate can refuse missing, contradictory, unsupported, or scenario-inconsistent critical-field restatements, request field-only correction, preserve an emergency-release bypass, and never grant authority. It cannot establish human comprehension.
- **T0 (no participants):** Freeze four disposable synthetic vignettes (uncertain delivery, partial edit, no-effect/stale target, emergency release) with oracle truth separate from the visible prompt. Run nine fixed responses: two correct, six planted incomplete/unsafe cases including packet-copy without scenario application, and one emergency no-readback case. Independently audit prompt/oracle separation, every decision, correction-field requests, and the safe-stop invariant. No human participants or sensitive data.
- **D:** `PASS_METHOD_SCOPED` only if all six planted incomplete/contradictory/unsafe responses HOLD with deterministic field-only correction requests; both correct responses proceed only to separate activation review; emergency release bypasses all synthesis requirements; oracle facts are not exposed to the candidate runner; and independent audit matches every row. Otherwise FAIL; HOLD if source truth or response rows cannot be independently audited. T0 does not test human understanding or benefit.
- **C:** A concise source-bound packet, direct inspection, or two-phase ownership acknowledgement may suffice; missing effect truth cannot be repaired by receiver synthesis. A correct verbatim response may still be rote, but a consistency checker cannot identify that latent mental state; it must not label it unsafe solely for being copied.
- **U:** Synthetic authored scenarios, deterministic categorical rules, no language variation, stress, fatigue, accessibility, or actual effect source. No participant, human-comprehension, takeover-quality, clinical, safety, or GUI-task claim. Emergency stop/release cannot wait for the gate.

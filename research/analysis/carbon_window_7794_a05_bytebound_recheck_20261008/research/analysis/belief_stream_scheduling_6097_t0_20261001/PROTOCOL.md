# Frozen T0 protocol — belief-conditioned optional-stream scheduling (#6097)

## H / T / D / C / U

- **H:** For at least one declared independent Markov-stream family with equal one-stream capture slots, a task-value × current-belief selector with a starvation cap strictly improves exact weighted misses versus cyclic, AoI, and highest-entropy scheduling on a preregistered held-out fixture. This is only a finite policy comparison, not an indexability or general scheduler claim. On coupling, mode-switch or unknown-transition controls, it must decline the belief/index claim and fall back to cyclic.
- **T:** Exact finite no-GUI/no-model Markov-world enumeration with full exogenous truth, fixed initial state, opportunity labels `state=1`, fixed task weights, equal horizon/capture budget, and identical mandatory sentinel stream. Compare cyclic, AoI, highest entropy, and belief×value with cap 3. Cases: independent construction family, independent held-out family, coupled/common-cause, mode-switch, unknown-transition. Enumerate all nonzero-probability world paths and action/outcome records; independently recompute raw scores. Whittle/indexability is not claimed or tested.
- **D:** `PASS_METHOD_SCOPED` only if held-out weighted misses strictly improve over all three baselines; capture counts equal; starvation≤3; false-confidence≤the frozen cap; exact probability mass is 1; mandatory-sentinel identity is unchanged; the exact p01=9/10, p10=2/5 belief recurrence shows age-3 entropy below age-2; unsupported controls choose cyclic and emit no index claim; mutation tests reject dropped worlds, altered labels, selective truth leakage, or false fallback claims. Otherwise report the exact FAIL/HOLD disposition; no tuning against held-out outcomes after freeze.
- **C:** Fully authored independent binary Markov streams and task weights. T0 opportunity labels are evaluator truth, not observed GUI labels. The policy receives only prior/current beliefs and selected-stream observations; the auditor sees all truth. `weight × P(opportunity)` is a simple myopic score, not a Whittle index or globally optimal policy.
- **U:** No empirical eligible GUI stream, no actual capture cost/timing, no live speed/quality/safety/human-tempo claim, no real transition estimate and no production scheduling recommendation. Because all outcomes are synthetic and full-state captures may jointly expose cues, live eligibility remains unestablished.

Horizon 4; three optional streams; one discretionary capture each tick; all methods receive the same mandatory sentinel (constant digest in every record). Independent streams use exact rational transition probabilities; outcomes and opportunity labels are fully enumerated. Mutation controls are pre-run tests.

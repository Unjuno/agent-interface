# Issue #6138 T0 — forward-equivalence sunk-cost discriminator

## H / T / D / C / U

- **H:** When every forward-looking operand is equal, a prior-spend-only change cannot rationally change the exact finite oracle choice; changing a prospective cost may change it. A paired-card checker must reject pairs with a real forward difference.
- **T:** One finite, no-model table: low/high prior irrecoverable spend (2 vs 200) with identical forward options; a positive control with future switch cost raised 2→4; six invalid-pair controls independently changing artifacts, future evidence, deadline, budget, safe-action set or future cost; and an UNKNOWN card with an unresolved success probability.
- **D:** `PASS_METHOD_SCOPED` only if exact payoff values and choices reconstruct, sink-only choice is invariant, positive control selects CONTINUE, all six forward-difference cards are rejected as matched pairs, and UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN; independent audit rejects three predeclared mutations.
- **C:** Exact arithmetic, authored payoff units, candidate and independent auditor each one-shot in separate pinned network-disabled OrbStack containers; no model, GUI, participant, live action, or user data.
- **U:** Synthetic decision-invariant/method evidence only. The payoff probabilities and units are stipulated, not calibrated. No claim that a model exhibits sunk-cost bias or that any route choice improves task success. This does not authorize T1.

## Frozen oracle

For each safe action, forward value = success probability × success reward − prospective action cost. Continue: 1/2 × 10 − 1 = 4. Switch: 7/10 × 10 − 2 = 5, so both low- and high-sunk cards choose SWITCH. Prior costs 2 and 200 are reported but excluded from the next marginal decision. In the positive control, only prospective switch cost becomes 4, so SWITCH=3 and CONTINUE=4; CONTINUE is the oracle choice. Any unresolved required input yields UNKNOWN.

## Scope limits

This is a deterministic no-model T0 method test, not T1. It establishes neither agent vulnerability nor defense efficacy, treatment effect, statistical power, strategic counterparty behavior, production-web robustness, actual forbidden effects, completion benefit, safety, or task success. The eight scenarios are constructed, not randomly sampled. The aggregate outcome difference is intentionally generated by the scripted agent condition and cannot be attributed to a real agent. A human/model/local-site evaluation needs its own held-out allocation, consent/fixture controls, and independent scoring.

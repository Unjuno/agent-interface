The A02 formal allocation is permanently `STOP_RUNNER_FAILURE`; its original frozen source hashes and raw failure are retained in the sibling A02 package. Its runner tried to create `/out`, although Docker had already mounted `/out`, and the capture exited before Chromium launch.

After A02 STOP, separate one-shot construction smokes found two runner-environment issues: the bind-mounted output directory must not be created by the entrypoint, and Playwright artifact/temp paths need an explicit writable `/tmp` plus `TMPDIR=/tmp` and `PLAYWRIGHT_ARTIFACTS_DIR=/tmp/pw-artifacts`. A direct mount smoke subsequently launched Chromium successfully.

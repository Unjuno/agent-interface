# Research archive

This is a retained historical archive namespace.

Files and directories under this path are kept in place because experiment provenance, reports, issues, commits, or audits may refer to their exact paths. Presence here does not imply that a result is promoted, current, or rejected.

For scientific status, use the top-level [research index](../../RESEARCH.md) and the report/audit inside the relevant experiment.

A second legacy namespace, [`../archives/`](../archives/), also exists. The two namespaces are intentionally documented rather than merged during repository cleanup so evidence paths remain stable.

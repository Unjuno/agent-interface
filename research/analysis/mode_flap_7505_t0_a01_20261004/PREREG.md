## Fixed simulator and comparator definitions

The fixture uses a 1 ms logical tick, 80 ticks/episode, a 3-unit demand baseline, six scheduled one-tick probes that add one demand unit, a protected safety lane serving one unit with zero safety backlog, queue-loss threshold 10 for three consecutive ticks, and an eight-tick rolling window. The positive family explicitly alternates source modes A/B on an accelerating seeded schedule, pays one service unit of transition overhead, and enters a persistent degraded mode at tick 52. Demand drift changes demand at ticks 20 and 40 without source-mode changes. The single-mode noise family holds actual service at 4 and demand at 3 while service telemetry samples `{2,5}`. Policy oscillation switches labels every two ticks while effective service remains 4. Abrupt failure changes service from 4 to 0 at tick 52 with no precursor. For that family, warning eligibility ends immediately before tick 52; a same-tick response to the abrupt change is not counted as a precursor warning. Stable remains at demand 3/service 4.

The sequential-shift comparator is positive Page-Hinkley-style queue CUSUM: `max(0, previous + queue_delta - 0.25)`. The #5776 comparator is the exact conceptual recovery ratio used in its T0-v2: latest completed fixed-probe recovery duration divided by first completed duration, available only after two recoveries. Margin deficit is the maximum positive `demand + probe - observed_service` over the rolling window. Mode-flap is the number of source-bound label changes in that window.

For each metric separately, take the maximum score over every eligible tick in every calibration episode from demand-drift, single-mode-noise, policy-oscillation, abrupt-failure and stable families. Abrupt-failure eligibility ends before its tick-52 jump; all other eligibility ends before the persistent-loss endpoint, when any. Alarm only when a score is strictly greater than that maximum. This fixes zero calibration alarms for each metric without consulting any held-out episode. Test false-alarm rate and warning lead are reported on all held-out episodes; no tuning or retry is allowed.

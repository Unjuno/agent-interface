## H / T / D / C / U

- **H:** After a timestamped trajectory residual and matching input-release receipt, one admissible read-only diagnostic observation will identify the correct recovery in the observation-path and dynamics-only cases. On the identifiable subset, bounded diagnosis will produce strictly fewer wrong recoveries and post-recovery residual recurrences than fixed reobserve and fixed reset, without forbidden transitions or delayed release. The mixed, ineligible, unsupported, or otherwise unclassified cases must YIELD. Immediate YIELD remains the conservative baseline and may have zero wrong recoveries but no useful effect.
- **T:** Use a frozen target trajectory and explicit linear predictor. Generate a timestamped pre-probe observation that crosses the residual threshold, a held-input lease with request/receipt IDs and event times, and at most one later numeric diagnostic observation with source and receive ticks. Candidate gets only trajectory/predictor/observations/lease evidence/signature-to-recovery map; hidden fault labels and post-recovery effect transitions are in a separate oracle for the raw-only auditor. Compare always reobserve, always reset, immediate YIELD, and bounded diagnosis. Enumerate every case×policy row; include observation-path, dynamics-only, mixed, probe-ineligible, and unsupported cases. Oracle records action-specific useful-effect outcome and post-recovery residual magnitude; the auditor independently reconstructs alarm, probe age/residual, release ordering, chosen action, wrong-recovery and recurrence counts, and worst-case release latency. Construction mutations swap numeric-signature mappings, corrupt the effect oracle, remove release evidence, and move a release receipt past probe time.
- **D:** `PASS_METHOD_SCOPED` only if the independent auditor agrees on all rows; diagnosis takes the correct recovery in both identifiable worlds and yields in mixed/ineligible/unsupported cases; every diagnostic probe follows a matching release receipt, uses a source tick no later than receive tick, occurs at the frozen probe tick, and respects the one-observation/non-effectful/no-lease-extension bound; inconsistent target/predictor trajectories and reversed event times fail closed; no forbidden transition occurs; and worst-case release latency is no worse than immediate YIELD. On the two preregistered identifiable cases, bounded diagnosis must have strictly fewer wrong recoveries and fewer post-recovery residuals above the frozen threshold than each fixed recovery arm. Immediate YIELD must not be misrepresented as worse on wrong-recovery count, and its useful-effect outcome is reported separately. `FAIL_METHOD` if a fixed recovery matches/beats either primary comparison or the diagnostic policy falsely isolates/acts on unknown evidence. `STOP` for candidate/auditor/runtime invocation failure; no retry or repair of a frozen formal invocation. No method outcome from construction tests.
- **C:** The diagnostic signatures and action-specific effect transitions are authored and may encode the expected answer; exact numeric matching is deliberately a method test, not a calibrated fault estimator. Always reobserve or always YIELD may be simpler and safer; a reset may suffice; mixed dynamics may be indistinguishable. The post-recovery residual is a separate simulated state outcome in the oracle, but its causal relationship to recovery is authored, not empirically identified.
- **U:** This deterministic finite model does not identify real GUI fault causes, validate signature likelihoods, establish a true OS input release, prove causal real-world recurrence reduction, measure latency distributions/usability, or authorize runtime changes/live actuation. The existing #5430 safe-probe matrix and #6147 distinguishing-sequence work are adjacent; this T0 tests one finite recovery-policy contrast, not a general safe-probe selector or production controller.

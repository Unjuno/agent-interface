# Issue #8598 T0 A01 — preregistered protocol

## H/T/D/C/U

**H — hypothesis.** In a finite, paired synthetic opportunity population, resolved-only upper-tail ranking is vulnerable to severity-dependent resolution. With correctly specified, positive completion probabilities, a known-propensity Hájek-weighted empirical upper-tail estimate will improve route-tail ranking versus resolved-only. Under zero/near-zero positivity or deliberately misspecified latent-severity censoring, a point ranking may be unsupported or wrong; assumption-free prefix/envelope partial bounds should contain the finite-population route-tail contrast and never certify a false unique ordering.

**T — test.** Deterministic CPU-only host experiment; no container, Docker/WSLc, network, GUI, model, humans, or live allocations. Six censoring arms × 128 paired cohorts × 32 opportunities = 768 cohort-arm instances and 24,576 assigned opportunities. Two routes, two equally weighted strata, horizon four, per-tick loss in [0,3], CVaR/expected-shortfall alpha=.75. The population route means are equal (2); population upper-tail ES is A=6, B=3. Candidate sees only assigned covariates, resolution, observed prefix, and declared completion probability, with resolved terminal values; the separate auditor receives hidden complete trajectories.

Arms: independent administrative completion (p=.70, correctly declared); recorded-covariate-dependent completion (p=.40/.90, correctly declared); severity-dependent completion (actual p=.05 for positive loss and .90 otherwise, while its displayed .70 weight is explicitly withheld/misspecified and the arm is descriptive only); zero positivity for one observed covariate stratum; near-zero p=.02 there; and no censoring. Fixed SHA-256-derived cohort sampling and completion/follow-up draws make construction deterministic.

**D — decision rule.** PASS_METHOD_SCOPED only if (1) exact public/truth denominator and prefix reconstruction pass; (2) an independent implementation reproduces every candidate stratum, macro, rank and status value within 1e-10; (3) all 768 finite-population oracle contrasts lie in the corresponding partial-tail intervals and there are zero false unique partial rankings; (4) zero and near-zero positivity arms emit no IPCW scalar/rank; (5) categorical hard-safety and missing-truth controls carry no numeric regret; (6) in the correctly declared recorded-covariate arm, strict correct IPCW tail-rank accuracy is at least 80% over 128 cohorts and strictly exceeds resolved-only; and (7) the latent-severity arm contains at least one resolved-only tail mis-rank and at least one overlapping/UNKNOWN partial rank. Otherwise report FAIL_METHOD or HOLD_AUDIT with exact evidence; do not tune or rerun after formal invocation. Construction exposed that the original 80%-plus-10pp rule fails; a preregistered amendment removed only the delta hurdle, retaining the 80% minimum and requiring strict paired improvement. The amended rule also failed deterministic construction screening (10/128 IPCW-correct, 20/128 resolved-only-correct). We preserve this as an adverse feasibility signal; we do not loosen the final scientific gate further. Candidate/auditor have not been invoked formally.

**C — counter-hypotheses.** Partial bounds can remain too wide to help; finite weighted tail estimates may be unstable or fail to improve rankings at this sample size; misspecified propensities can make IPCW worse; deterministic paired patterns may expose only these authored cases. Any such result is retained as-is and limits or rejects the hypothesis.

**U — use boundary.** This is a deterministic synthetic finite-population method test only. It does not establish real GUI, user, model, workflow, causal, safety, or product performance. The implemented IPCW/Hájek weighted empirical CVaR is not the censored expected-shortfall regression estimator from the cited literature. No empirical claim beyond this frozen fixture.

## Estimands and analysis

For each route and stratum, ES is the equal-opportunity upper-tail average of cumulative four-tick regret at alpha .75; route estimand is the unweighted mean of the two stratum ES values. Resolved-only uses completed opportunities alone. IPCW uses completed outcomes with weights 1/p_declared, Hájek-normalized for means and weighted upper-tail mass for ES; any assigned propensity below .05 makes that route-stratum point estimate unsupported. This is assumption-dependent and is not claimed to identify arbitrary informative censoring.

For each opportunity with visible prefix sum L_t, follow-up t and per-tick cap M=3, the assumption-free terminal interval is [L_t, L_t+(4-t)M]. Route-stratum ES lower/upper endpoints apply monotonicity to these per-opportunity endpoint vectors; the two strata are macro-averaged. A route contrast interval is [A_lower−B_upper, A_upper−B_lower]. A unique route order is emitted only when zero is excluded. Full hidden truth is used only by the independent auditor to test finite-population coverage and false certification.

Its earlier mutation moves the decision timestamp earlier; it does not test a same-time ordering reversal.

Accordingly, the retained trace sequence cannot independently establish observation-before-decision ordering for simultaneous events. These ties do not by themselves establish that the candidate used a cue before delivery: its declared construction sets decision time to the completed observation time.

# Frozen H / T / D / C / U

**H.** On the scripted stable turn, version-bound read-only preparation
overlapped with final-utterance processing and reduced declared time to
verified effect versus final-only processing. On revision/ambiguity/stop
controls, it produces the same final-only effect disposition with no
provisional, stale, duplicate, or unauthenticated consequential event.

**T.** Enumerate 10 fixed event scenarios under FINAL_ONLY,
NAIVE_PROVISIONAL_EFFECT, and VERSION_BOUND_READ_ONLY_PREPARATION (30 rows).
The independent auditor replays final-principal/force gates, source epochs,
event/effect boundaries, synthetic timing, and release. Four mutations test
old-epoch reuse, partial-as-final, speaker merging, and treating STOP as undo.

**D.** PASS_METHOD_SCOPED requires exact independent reconstruction; stable
version-bound preparation must save positive declared time; version-bound
outcomes and issued action sequence must equal final-only on every scenario;
no invalid/provisional action, duplicate, lost release, or false undo; and all
four mutations rejected. A scoped H pass applies only to the scripted stable
turn and declared timing; it is not real ASR or UX latency.

**C.** Final-only may be sufficiently fast; safe preparation may add no
material benefit; #6471/#6424 gates may dominate; effect uncertainty may be
more important than dialogue timing.

**U.** Scripted text revisions do not represent audio, ASR, prosody, overlap
detection, or human intent. Timings are simulator quantities. No real
voice/GUI route, participant, model, task outcome, or safety claim.

No audio/network/model/GUI/live input. Host standard-library Python is
sufficient. Candidate and auditor each run once after preregistration; retries
are zero.

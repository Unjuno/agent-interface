## Design

The previous result demonstrated a useful distinction only inside a fixture where policy and oracle were co-authored. This successor tests the measurement decomposition against a separately written case generator and auditor, with held-out seeds and blinded-to-stratum policy inputs. It remains a deterministic synthetic method test; “independent” means separate implementations/modules and input/output custody, not independent human laboratories.

Generate 48 paired cases (four fixed seeds 17/29/41/53 × six evidence strata × two repetitions) from a declarative evidence/action schema. Candidate policies receive only observable evidence state and the allowed action/effect catalog, never the generator's stratum label or oracle outcome. Include acquisition, representation transformation, discriminating probe, sufficient-evidence stop, unsafe/uninformative action, and unsupported UNKNOWN strata. Compare a reference policy with four deterministic planted failure policies—recognition, action selection, evidence use, and stopping—under prescribed, available, and no-epistemic-action arms (720 total rows). Compare competency profiles with aggregate task outcome alone.

The separate auditor reads only retained generated cases, policy decisions, and outcome receipts. It recomputes admissibility, evidence transitions, decision outcomes, and the per-dimension scorecard without importing generator or policy code. Held-out seeds are fixed in this preregistration; every generated byte is bound to seed and SHA-256 before the candidate and auditor are frozen.

## H / T / D / C / U

- **H:** On held-out finite synthetic cases, policy failure profiles that can share aggregate task success remain distinguishable by the preregistered competency ledger, without hard-gate violations.
- **T:** Generate a fixed set of held-out seeds × six evidence strata. Compare PRESCRIBED, AVAILABLE, and NO_EPISTEMIC_ACTION for independent policy implementations. Record recognition, action choice, evidence production, evidence use, stopping, task result, cost, provenance, and safety/authority gates. Compare diagnostic distinctions with task outcome alone. Add hostile raw/case mutations.
- **D:** PASS_METHOD_SCOPED only if held-out case binding is complete, the raw-only auditor reconstructs all 720 case-policy-arm rows, and all four preregistered corruption controls are rejected. H_PASS_SCOPED only if all four planted competency defects are detected on held-out cases and STOPPING_DEFECT shares aggregate task success with REFERENCE while scoring worse on stopping, with zero hard-gate violations. H_FAIL_SCOPED if those predeclared diagnostic gates fail. HOLD if generator/oracle/policy leakage, missing rows, or held-out binding cannot be audited.
- **C:** Deterministic planted policy defects can still favor a hand-designed scorecard. A finite family does not establish construct validity or real-agent discrimination; direct outcomes may remain sufficient in practice.
- **U:** Synthetic method evidence only. No model/human/general competence, GUI, privacy, runtime safety, user benefit, latency, or product claim. No real-world probe or consequential action.

## Formal controls

Write tests before generator, policy, oracle, or auditor code. Verify RED against absent entrypoints, then GREEN. Freeze generator inputs/seeds/output, oracle, candidate, auditor, tests, and runner with SHA-256 before formal launch. One candidate invocation and one separate auditor invocation; no retry. Preserve every setup failure, formal outcome, and stop with exact bytes. Use the host directly for this small pure CPU fixture; do not invoke Docker/WSLc absent a concrete isolation need.

## Open design risk

“Independent” authorship within one research packet is weaker than blinded external authorship. If input/output separation and audit cannot establish the limited separation claimed here, stop and record HOLD rather than overstate it.

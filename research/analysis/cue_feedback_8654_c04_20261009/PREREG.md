## H/T/D/C/U

**H — falsifiable hypothesis.** Complete observed action support is not sufficient to guarantee correct cue-regime classification for every positive-probability finite realization under the frozen Horvitz–Thompson threshold rule. In the stable regime, at least one fully supported tuple will be labeled GLOBAL_SHIFT because the finite-sample HT cue mean is at or below the strict 0.5 threshold.

**T — frozen test.** Two equiprobable contexts, 16 trials/context, two already-qualified actions, known alternative-action propensity p=0.25. Enumerate exactly all 17×17 binomial count tuples for three deterministic regimes (STABLE cue=1/alternative=0; REVERSAL 0/1; GLOBAL_SHIFT 0/−1), plus one explicit GREEDY_CUE_ONLY baseline row per regime. Each diagnostic row weight is Binom(16,k0;0.25)×Binom(16,k1;0.25); total opportunities remain fixed. Apply the frozen HT means and decision rule: require both actions in both contexts, then UNIDENTIFIABLE without support; REVERSAL if cue-minus-alternative <0; else STABLE if cue HT mean >0.5; else GLOBAL_SHIFT.

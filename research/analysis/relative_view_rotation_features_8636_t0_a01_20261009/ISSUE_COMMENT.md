This is the preregistration for a bounded CPU-only T0 A01 experiment for #8636. It compares raw normalized landmark coordinates, a three-bearing spherical feature controller, and a hidden-pose oracle on 30 paired seeds in each of four strata. The feature-loss stratum contains nine deterministic controls: missing landmark, identity swap, moved landmark, near-singular geometry, low texture, camera translation, stale generation, viewport resize, and radial range variation (a perspective-projection null). The image-only arms must abstain on the eight invalid conditions; the radial range null must preserve the image.

The primary hypothesis is limited to this synthetic fixed-center rotation fixture: spherical features preserve exact terminal success and target-loss rate while reducing median corrections by at least 20% versus raw coordinates on the 60 rotation-only/combined pairs. Focal shift and fault controls are separate stress findings. No live GUI, game, OS input, model, GUI automation, latency, task-effect, safety, or product claim is included.

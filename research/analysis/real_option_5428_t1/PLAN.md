# T1 plan — Issue #5428 real-option commit timing

## H/T/D/C/U

- **H:** In a finite uncertain-effect model, an option-aware one-step policy that prices the route set remaining after a safe probe will have lower expected regret than safety-only and VOI-only policies on costly-to-reverse/one-shot cases, without exceeding a 5% probe-induced primary-route deadline-loss rate. Hard-gate violations must remain zero.
- **T:** One deterministic, exhaustive factorial container run: 4 irreversibility classes × 3 bad-state priors × 3 probe accuracies × 2 wait costs × 2 deadline states × 2 immediate-alternative states × 2 post-wait alternative states × 2 primary-route-survival states × 2 hard-gate states = 2,304 scenarios. Each scenario has two latent target states and both possible probe signals. Compare a safety-only expected-utility threshold, a VOI-only probe trigger that ignores post-wait route changes, and an option-aware one-step policy that evaluates the post-probe surviving action set. An exact rational reference enumerates the same legal immediate/probe actions. One action benefit is 2; bad-state loss is 1/4, 1/2, 1, or 2 by reversibility class; the safe alternative yields 3/4; probe accuracy is 1/2, 3/4, or 9/10; waiting costs 1/4 or 3/4. A probe consumes the only available unit of deadline slack. No RNG, external data, model, GPU, or retries.
- **D:** PASS for the model-level claim only if option-aware regret is at least 1/20 lower than each baseline in the pooled costly-to-reverse/one-shot subset, probe-induced loss of a primary route is ≤5% of that subset, and hard-gate violations are zero. FAIL if any threshold misses or a hard gate is bypassed. Even PASS is `UNCERTAIN` for runtime transfer because priors, utilities, route-survival facts, and probe quality are authored. The option-aware arm is an explicit one-step lookahead comparator, not a deployable valuation algorithm.
- **C:** VOI-only may already capture the useful timing decision; a conservative HOLD or the exact full-state oracle may dominate an ordinal option ledger. Apparent benefit may come entirely from hand-authored route survival or payoff assumptions.
- **U:** This deterministic one-probe model does not establish calibrated utilities/probabilities, human preferences, realistic route availability, temporal hysteresis, adversarial hidden effects, fairness, or live GUI/tool behavior. Hysteresis is deliberately outside T1; a later rung would require a separately frozen multi-observation policy test. No financial or product-safety claim.

## Frozen policy definitions

- **SAFETY_ONLY:** with the hard gate open, choose the highest expected-utility action currently available (primary commit, immediate alternative, or no-op); do not probe.
- **VOI_ONLY:** compute expected value of sample information for the primary action alone. Probe iff that value strictly exceeds wait cost and deadline slack permits; after the probe, choose among only the routes that actually survive.
- **OPTION_AWARE:** enumerate the exact expected value of a probe followed by the actual surviving post-wait action set, including the alternative route; choose that plan only when it strictly improves on the best current action. This is intentionally a small-model upper-bound comparator.
- **REFERENCE:** independently enumerate legal immediate and probe branches under the same stated model, then take the maximum. It is an audit reference, not a treatment.

The formal candidate is one invocation after preregistration. Construction/syntax checks use only a separate single hand-authored smoke case, never the factorial formal grid. Preserve candidate output, independent audit, and corruption-control output byte-for-byte.

the frozen allocation. `FREEZE.json`, `RUN.json`, raw candidate/auditor output,

## Finding

The frozen preregistration's decision rule requires that all four listed output
mutations be submitted to the independent auditor and rejected. The frozen
`auditor.py` instead constructs mutated copies and sets each
`mutations_rejected[...]` value to a direct comparison such as
`forged != expected`. Because each constructed copy differs from `expected`,
these booleans become true without calling `audit(fixture, mutated_rows)` or
recording a rejection result. Thus the formal `RUN.json` and `audit.json`
mutation flags do not demonstrate the preregistered mutation criterion.

The checked source is the exact frozen auditor: its Git blob is
`b43df706b8c9d9cbb8ed11564fb5c539be1d20b9`, unchanged from source commit
`142ed4c653f8421610b2f230ec731963c5309b18`, and its SHA-256 matches
`FREEZE.json` (`8dafa13b8db3934be5c72a28361629e42fc6403c2779582d5433fc2038a3ff6c`).

The construction suite does call `audit(fixture, forged)` for one tampered
classification. That pre-freeze construction test is not execution of all four
frozen formal output mutations. An independent raw-only review later
reconstructed 31/31 expected rows and the three machine-only tuples from the
authored fixture; its stated 12-state decision-rule check also does not repair
the missing four-mutant formal audit.

## Corrected disposition

`T0_GATE_NOT_ESTABLISHED_MUTATION_EXECUTION_MISSING` supersedes the package's
and issue catalog's `PASS_METHOD_SCOPED_T0_ONLY` acceptance label. The row
reconstruction and the three machine-only tuples remain scoped evidence about
the authored finite model. This correction does not demonstrate a classifier
mislabel, a real filesystem state, or a host durability defect; it means the
frozen conjunctive decision rule was not fully evidenced. The original formal

Every pair shares load, mechanism, phase-arrival tick, capacity/demand schedule, horizon and endpoint; only the probe policy differs. Mechanisms: gradual capacity loss, gradual capacity decline stopping above demand, demand drift with fixed capacity, abrupt capacity loss, and stable no-loss. No model, GUI, network, external service, or live task. Run candidate once and a separate raw-only replay auditor once in distinct network-disabled containers.

The independent loss endpoint is backlog strictly above `2 × base_load` for two consecutive ticks; it is not derived from any warning statistic. The target is eligible only if all 18 target no-probe arms and all 18 paired probe arms cross this endpoint within the frozen 96-tick horizon, and every no-probe target onset is later than tick 48 (the last scheduled probe opportunity). A probe-arm onset may occur earlier; that is the measured intervention effect, and no subsequent probe is applied after the endpoint is reached. After a zero-error replay, report exact paired onset advances (`no_probe.loss_tick − probe.loss_tick`), loss incidence and backlog-area deltas by mechanism/load. `PASS_PROBE_ADVANCES_ENDPOINT_IN_FIXTURE` requires eligible target and median paired advance ≥4 ticks; otherwise `NO_MATERIAL_PROBE_ADVANCE_IN_FIXTURE`.

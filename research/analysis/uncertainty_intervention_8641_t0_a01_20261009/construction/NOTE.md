# Construction attempts

The first construction output at `construction/candidate.json` and `construction/audit.json` is retained as the initial smoke attempt. Its audit incorrectly classified scientific H-gate misses as audit errors; this was corrected before the formal freeze. The candidate and audit sources were then separated into an integrity disposition and an H disposition.

Attempts 02–03 retain prototype cue/decision-value fixtures and outputs before the final observation-feature-only source estimator and corrected misspecified oracle label. They are construction diagnostics and are not pooled.

`attempt_04/` is the final disjoint construction. Its independent audit reconstructed 480 rows with zero audit errors; source-classifier accuracy was 5/6, and the hypothesis gate failed because mean cost was not reduced against either comparator at matched correctness. Label, dependence, stale-epoch action, and cost mutations were rejected (4/4). Formal inputs and outcome are recorded separately at the package root.

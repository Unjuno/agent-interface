**T.** Enumerate nine deterministic scenarios and six binary items: two anchors, a null, uniform-DIF target, nonuniform-DIF target, and low-discrimination target. Scenarios cover invariant and composition-only controls, uniform and nonuniform DIF, placebo labels, low discrimination, no common support, invalid common anchors, and missing/UNKNOWN outcomes. The missingness case retains 90 null outcomes in its denominator. Candidate reports crude group marginal means, within-stratum contrasts, and item classifications. This is a deterministic sensitivity fixture; it does not fit a generalized logistic model or estimate population uncertainty.

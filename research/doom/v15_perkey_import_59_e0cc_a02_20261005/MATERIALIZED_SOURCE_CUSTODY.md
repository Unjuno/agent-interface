# Materialized source copy custody

Before packaging, the independent PowerShell readback
`MATERIALIZED_SOURCE_AUDIT.json` verified all 56 materialized files for both
attempt 01 and attempt 02 byte-for-byte against `source-manifest.json` (zero
mismatches). `AUDIT_REPAIR_01.json` independently records the same 56/56
match for the successful run.

The two `results/*/source/` trees were generated by the replay wrapper from
the retained 56-file `source-snapshots/` set. They contained no unique bytes,
and added 112 duplicate source files to the Draft PR. After recording both
readbacks, the duplicate materializations were removed from the published
package. The exact source bytes, their manifest, the runner that rematerializes
them, all route outputs, and both independent readback results remain. The
replay wrapper creates a fresh source tree for any reproduction.

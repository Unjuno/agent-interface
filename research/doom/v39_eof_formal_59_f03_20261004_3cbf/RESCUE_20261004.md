# F03 construction and custody history rescue

This additive package preserves 55 source-branch files that were absent from
main after the F03 formal result was integrated separately by PR #7373. It
contains construction tests, successive review/fix records, preflight and
interruption STOP evidence, custody procedures, and their raw method logs.
These records are historical; no producer, formal allocation, or auditor was
rerun for this rescue.

## Evidence boundary

- The preserved methods report `PASS_CONSTRUCTION_ONLY`, not a formal producer
  or official-auditor result. Their explicit native/official-auditor/model
  counts remain zero except where the separate #7373 record says otherwise.
- Review records retain unresolved Important findings and state that the
  package was not ready for another formal launch. This rescue does not imply
  those gates were subsequently closed.
- The formal result and its receipts remain in the distinct main-tree package
  integrated by #7373; this rescue does not replace or modify them.
- Raw container logs, reported image/archive digests, and custody artifacts
  are preserved as source evidence, not independently re-attested here.

The 55 rescued files are copied byte-for-byte from
`research/v39-eof-f02-3cbf-20261004`; only this qualification note and the
navigation link are new. No claim of production adoption, gameplay effect, or
end-to-end lifecycle validation is made.

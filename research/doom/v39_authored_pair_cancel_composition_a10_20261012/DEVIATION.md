- Issue #59 remains open and unassigned, so no live Doom lane exists. This construction test cannot replace the required fresh threat exposure.

- The planner client and executor are fakes. A synthetic terminal receipt satisfies the helper's schema check but does not represent delivered OS key-up events, physical release, or application consumption.
- WSLc warned that swap-limit capabilities were unavailable (or cgroup was not mounted); 512 MiB was therefore requested without swap. A separate probe observed `cpu.max=100000 100000` and `memory.max=536870912`. No stronger isolation/resource claim is made.
- The receipt leaves `latest_observation_sequence=10` because the fixture supplies a typed invalidation and terminal only; it does not publish the later matching full-frame observation. Source refresh and useful feedback were outside this experiment.

# Recording-salience reactivity in human interface evaluations

- **Status:** Unverified evaluation-validity proposal; no human study.
- **Source:** Issue [#8022](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8022).
- **Idea:** Among fully informed and consenting participants, test the incremental effect of recording/evaluation reminders on behavior without comparing consent with covert observation.
- **Outcome:** No participants, recording, or study were authorized or conducted. The proposed estimand concerns reminder salience among consented participants; it is not a claim that operators currently change behavior when recorded.

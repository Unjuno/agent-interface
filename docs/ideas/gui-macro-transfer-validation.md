# GUI macro transfer and translation validation

These Issues concern reusable procedures but ask different questions. Their results must remain separate, and none authorizes live macro execution.

## Functional representativeness under transfer — #8890

**Status:** Open, unverified transfer and evaluation-method proposal.

**Idea.** Test whether a relational, source-bound GUI macro transfers when appearance changes but task-relevant cue/action/effect relations stay fixed, and abstains when those functional relations change. The proposed finite 2×2 design crosses near-source versus changed appearance with preserved versus changed cue/effect coupling. Include visually identical but functionally changed cases and visually changed but function-preserving cases; score verified effect, wrong-effect dispatch, abstention, and steps against an independent oracle.

The proposal borrows a test-design concept from representative learning design; it does not claim that sport-learning mechanisms transfer to software. It is not a screenshot-similarity benchmark alone, and no current macro overfitting or generalization failure is asserted. No result is reported. See [Issue #8890](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8890).

## Per-artifact source/compiled equivalence — #7827 and #8869

**#7827 retained boundary.** Its A05 finite method result reports five valid source/target pairs accepted, seven registered mutations rejected, and an oversized repeat returned UNKNOWN without a certificate. This is scoped to its frozen source/target context corpus; it does not include an independently scheduled external GUI actor. Earlier allocation outcomes and custody limitations remain preserved. See [Issue #7827](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/7827).

**#8869 proposed successor.** Compare one frozen source procedure and its compiled macro against the same explicit environment-event schedules. Keep focus/window replacement, target insertion/removal, modals, cancellation, lease revocation, and application completion as external events, not atomic or hidden macro steps. Check visible outcome relations and hard invariants, and compare DPOR classifications with exhaustive schedules on the finite oracle model.

**Status:** #8869 is open and unverified. It preserves #7827 A01–A05 unchanged and grants no macro execution authority. Matching traces in an authored model cannot establish real GUI event completeness, effect truth, or safety. See [Issue #8869](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8869).

## Boundary between the proposals

#8890 asks whether the declared cue/effect relation remains representative across a family of variants. #8869 asks whether a particular compiled artifact preserves its source semantics under exogenous schedules. A later study could compose both only with explicit registration of each question, its oracle, and its failure criteria; neither currently supplies the other's evidence.

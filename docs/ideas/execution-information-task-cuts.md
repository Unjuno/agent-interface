# Execution-information requirements at GUI task cuts

- **Status:** unverified transfer proposal; finite method result held
- **Source:** [Issue #8620](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8620), successor to [Issue #5329](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/5329)
- **Idea:** at each cut in a stateful GUI task, partition past histories by whether any allowed future input requires a different output. Measure the minimum distinctions to retain, the evidence fields carrying them, and whether missing information can be safely reacquired at lower cost. Preserve lineage, freshness, authority, release, and effect proof as evidence obligations; a compact task summary cannot replace them.
- **Closest work:** #5329 tests decision-sufficient evidence compression; #746 selects historical frames; #5368 represents hidden alternatives for action choice; #5306 studies verifier stopping. This proposal asks for a cut-wise lower bound under an explicit future-task contract plus recovery cost. The relationship is a scoped comparison, not a repository-wide novelty claim.
- **First outcome:** T0 A01 ran once over 12 finite histories and two contracts. The partition expanded 3→12; minimum retained fields changed from `selection` (24 synthetic bits) to `saved, selection, lineage, freshness, authority` (132 synthetic bits). EIR had zero unavailable requirements; fixed-window and task-summary policies had 4 and 5 in contract_v2. The comparison is finite, authored, and synthetic.
- **Disposition:** `HOLD_FORMAL_FREEZE_INCOMPLETE`. Frozen auditor v1's `FAIL` remains unchanged. Raw-only diagnostic v2 confirmed fixture predicates and 3/3 mutation rejection, but exact CPython 3.14.5 was observed post-run, so it cannot repair the pre-execution freeze. The candidate was not rerun. The preserved packet and all checksums are in [T0 A01](../../research/analysis/execution_information_8620_t0_a01_20261009/README.md); the post-result ruling is [here](../../research/analysis/execution_information_8620_t0_a01_20261009/POST_RESULT_ADJUDICATION.md). Current-main successor [PR #9296](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/pull/9296) carries the packet and index entries; stale-base [PR #8716](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/pull/8716) is superseded after exact evidence transfer.
- **Limits:** no model, GUI, network, live memory pressure, latency, task-effect, safety, or product result. T1 remains conditional and unrun.
- **Next decision:** keep #8620 open for its cross-domain evaluation question. Only consider T1 after a new prospective freeze pins the exact runtime before execution, defines an independent effect oracle and reacquisition affordances, and identifies a fresh authorized allocation. Do not reuse the consumed T0 allocation.

# Incremental maintenance of nonmonotone GUI claims — #8917

- **Status:** Unverified finite-method proposal; no allocation or result is recorded.
- **Source:** Open [Issue #8917](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8917), successor at the intersection of [#2305](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/2305), [#4431](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/4431), and [#8624](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8624).
- **Idea:** On a small stratified claim graph, compare full recomputation with positive-only support updates and stratified delete-and-rederive after mixed fact insertions and deletions. Bind negative/cardinality conclusions to explicit scope, epoch, and completeness; stale, incomplete, or cross-epoch evidence must yield `UNKNOWN`, never certified absence.
- **Overlap:** #8624 asks which frozen signed facts make a negative-query outcome pivotal; #8917 asks how an already-scoped negative or aggregate claim is retracted and rederived after updates. #5865 owns query-scope completeness, which remains a prerequisite. #4431 covers positive cyclic grounding/deletion; #2305 covers broader live GUI justification maintenance. Keep their prior evidence and dispositions unchanged.
- **Proposed discriminator:** Exhaustively compare a candidate incremental maintainer with an independently implemented full-rebuild oracle across matching-target and blocker inserts/deletes, alternative support changes, unrelated updates, epoch replacement, and complete-to-incomplete transitions. Audit claim vectors, stale-TRUE retention, UNKNOWN behavior, mutation detection, and affected work. Treat work counts as synthetic only.
- **Boundary:** Even a method PASS would establish only the frozen finite model and rules. It would not prove GUI scans complete/current, live latency or memory benefit, action safety, or product readiness. No candidate execution, retry, or runtime-policy change is recorded.

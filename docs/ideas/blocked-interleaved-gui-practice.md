# Blocked versus interleaved GUI practice

- **Status:** Synthetic method readiness passed; human learning remains untested.
- **Source:** Issue [#8080](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8080).
- **Idea:** Compare blocked and interleaved schedules for later retention and transfer of GUI procedures while keeping task exposure and feedback constant.
- **Outcome:** A01 `METHOD_PASS_SCOPED` validated a synthetic schedule fixture with equal exposure and mutation controls. It measured no participants, delayed retention, GUI transfer, safety, or product effect. The proposed human study remains separately governed and is not authorized by the method result.

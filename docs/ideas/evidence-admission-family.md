- **Status:** related research questions; finite contract results exist, but operational admission remains unverified.
- **Sources:** [#5305](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/5305), [#5314](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/5314), and [#5339](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/5339). ## How the pieces compose

Treat these as three separate checks in order: (1) #5314 describes which evidence sources may count as distinct; (2) #5305 preserves whether those sources support, refute, conflict, or leave ignorance; (3) #5339 decides whether the resulting trustworthy and in-scope coverage is sufficient for a policy decision. A PASS at any one layer does not grant action authority. Unknown lineage stays unknown; conflict stays distinct from ignorance; missing trust/coverage must not become PASS by relaxation.

The related ideas are not duplicates. #5314's quorum result assumes an explicitly stipulated dependency universe, #5305's conflict model assumes freshness/independence metadata for its finite fixture, and #5339's singleton exception assumes truthful issuer/provenance/freshness/scope labels. Those assumptions do not validate one another.

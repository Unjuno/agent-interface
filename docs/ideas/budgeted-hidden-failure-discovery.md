# Budgeted discovery of hidden failures across task cells — #8914

- **Status:** Research proposal remains scientifically unverified. Two synthetic allocations, A01 and A02, are consumed with `HOLD_AUDITOR_METHOD_FAILURE`; neither produced a hypothesis PASS or FAIL.
- **Source:** Open [Issue #8914](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8914); retained allocation records in open [PR #9007](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/pull/9007).
- **Idea:** Under a fixed repeat budget, test whether metadata or shallow-outcome ranking discovers more hidden failures among held-out task cells with zero shallow failures than uniform deep sampling. A cell includes a frozen task, initial application state/version, model/configuration, and route; eligible repeats require an independently verified deterministic reset. Discovery yield is not population-risk estimation or safety certification.
- **A01:** Candidate/runner executed once and emitted 61,800 deep-attempt rows across 103 synthetic worlds and three arms. The independent auditor exited 1 at `sentinel_not_reproducible` before scientific gates because runner and auditor used different sentinel ordering. The allocation is consumed; no mutation controls ran.
- **A02:** Candidate/runner and auditor each executed once, with the same row/world/arm counts. The auditor again exited 1 at `sentinel_not_reproducible`; runner RNG state and auditor's fresh-RNG reconstruction did not implement the same sentinel contract. This allocation is also consumed; no mutation controls ran.

Do not repair or retry either allocation ID; these outcomes say nothing scientific about ranking efficacy.

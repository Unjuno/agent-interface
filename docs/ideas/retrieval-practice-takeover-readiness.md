# Retrieval practice during low-demand gaps

- **Status:** Synthetic instrument validation passed; participant/live-user study remains unrun and separately gated.
- **Source:** Issue [#8498](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8498), successor to [#5830](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/5830).
- **Idea:** Compare brief retrieval practice during predeclared low-demand gaps with matched restudy, measuring later takeover readiness while accounting for distraction and missed automation failures.
- **Outcome:** T0 validated a finite synthetic instrument on 18 generated rows; a separate raw-only truth-table audit passed and all five corruption controls were rejected. This does not establish human learning, operational takeover benefit, GUI behavior, or low-demand detection. T1 requires its own ethics, consent, privacy, and allocation review. Merged [PR #8499](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/pull/8499) also corrected two hash-manifest path/transcription errors without changing the result.

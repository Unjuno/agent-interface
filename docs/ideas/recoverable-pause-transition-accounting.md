# Recoverable pauses as within-task transitions — #8902

- **Status:** Unverified transfer and evaluation-method proposal; no allocation or result is recorded.
- **Source:** Open [Issue #8902](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8902), including its scope clarification on [#8068](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8902#issuecomment-6125763201).
- **Idea:** Represent a single assigned task episode's recoverable pauses and recovery cycles as transient states, distinct from absorbing success, failure, safe stop, abandonment, or administrative loss. Report success by the original deadline and time spent in running/recovery states so successful recovery does not erase its cost.
- **Relation to prior work:** #5593 handles first-terminal competing outcomes and identifies this multi-state gap. #5962 concerns survival across task sequences; #7042 concerns recovery cure/reset scope; #30 concerns observation-only recovery. #8068 asks whether repair/reset history changes recurrence risk. Its finite method pass and empirical-history HOLD do not test #8902's transition accounting. Reuse compatible event identity, timestamps, censoring conventions, and oracle practices, while keeping estimands and results separate.
- **Proposed minimum test:** On complete synthetic event histories, compare exact transition counts, deadline-success proportions, terminal causes, and restricted time-in-state against an independent raw-only auditor. Include repeated pauses, recovery failure, terminal safe stop, wrong-effect failure, abandonment, censoring, and mutations that absorb a transient pause, drop an interval, alter a clock, or remove an assigned episode.
- **Decision boundary:** A scoped pass requires exact reconstruction of transitions, denominator, deadline success, and occupancy, and rejection of lossy summaries on targeted counterexamples. Do not infer real recovery benefit, Markov validity, or a safety guarantee from synthetic accounting.
- **Limits:** Historical mixed allocations are not a qualifying cohort. Real use requires same-episode identity, independently supported timestamps/transitions, final-effect scoring, and censoring provenance. If no eligible cohort exists, retain a HOLD rather than infer transitions.

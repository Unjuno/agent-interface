# Functional search diversity in failure diagnosis — #8901

- **Status:** Unverified transfer and evaluation-method proposal; no investigation allocation or result is recorded.
- **Source:** Open [Issue #8901](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8901).
- **Idea:** Compare a fixed-budget portfolio of complementary diagnostic search lenses with repeated use of the strongest single lens and an unstructured baseline, on identical immutable failure traces. The target is evidence-linked hypothesis coverage before a causal catalog is complete, not a diagnosis vote or authority mechanism.
- **Relation to prior work:** #5430 derives diagnoses from already extracted evidence conflicts; #8894 designs tests for a predeclared fault catalog; #3177 ranks causal events for context; #5314/#5941 address verifier or peer-judgment independence. #8901 studies early hypothesis-space coverage under a matched analysis budget. Hong–Page's search model is conditional prior art, not proof that diverse prompts improve GUI diagnosis; classifier-diversity results warn against optimizing disagreement alone.
- **Proposed minimum test:** On finite held-out traces with independently planted single/multiple causes and out-of-catalog cases, compare HOMOGENEOUS-BEST, a calibration-selected DIVERSE-PORTFOLIO, and an UNRESTRICTED-BASELINE at equal model, token, call, and adjudication budgets. Seal first-pass outputs; score evidence IDs, causal recall, unique supported mechanisms, unsupported specificity, UNKNOWN handling, duplicates, and cost with an independent raw auditor.
- **Decision boundary:** A scoped hypothesis-support result requires higher held-out causal recall than the best single lens, within a preregistered unsupported-specificity margin, while out-of-catalog cases remain UNKNOWN and all budgets/blinding are audited. Ties or losses support no incremental value; diversity or disagreement alone is not success.
- **Limits:** Planted finite causes do not establish real-world prevalence or open-world discovery. Same-model lenses may share errors; outputs cannot become causal facts by consensus. No live GUI, action authority, safety, speed, or product-benefit claim follows.

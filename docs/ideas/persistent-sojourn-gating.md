# Persistent-sojourn gating for optional evidence generation

- **Status:** #8891 consolidated into canonical open Issue #6650; the broader synthetic comparison has a recorded `FAIL_HYPOTHESIS`.
- **Source:** [Issue #8891](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8891), closed as duplicate/consolidation into [Issue #6650](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/6650).
- **Idea retained from #8891:** Use recent-minimum queue sojourn as a candidate persistence signal for reducing generation of redundant optional observations/probes, while leaving already-admitted records and mandatory work intact. This is an estimator refinement within #6650, not an independent hypothesis or allocation.
- **Existing #6650 result:** The 2026-10-02 synthetic T0 reported `FAIL_HYPOTHESIS`. On sustained overload, stale optional deliveries were 13 for fixed generation, 3 for queue-length gating, and 6 for age-persistence gating. Across nine traces, queue-length had 11 tier transitions and age-persistence 16; the report says age-persistence avoided throttling the transient burst and reacted during a no-service blackout, but these scoped properties did not outweigh the adverse comparisons. Mandatory IDs and terminal outcomes matched fixed generation. The independent raw auditor had zero accounting/freshness errors; it did not independently replay the control law.
- **Boundary:** Preserve the negative result and its audit limitation. Any estimator change belongs inside #6650's existing comparison and needs an explicit discriminator; do not present #8891 as a separate replication or as evidence of live-agent benefit.

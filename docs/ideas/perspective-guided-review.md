# Perspective-guided review for cross-invariant omissions — #8574

- **Status:** The frozen T0 is complete with `NO_INCREMENTAL_VALUE_SCOPED`; broader transfer remains inconclusive and deferred.
- **Source:** [Issue #8574](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8574), successor to [#8088](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/8088); retained packet at [`research/analysis/perspective_guided_review_8574_t0_a01_20261009/REPORT.md`](../../research/analysis/perspective_guided_review_8574_t0_a01_20261009/REPORT.md).
- **Idea:** Test whether structured perspective-guided review of explicit task and verification contracts finds cross-invariant omissions that ordinary checklist review and free-form decomposition both miss, without inventing requirements or collapsing ambiguity.
- **Outcome:** Six same-family Codex reviewers assessed 16 synthetic contracts, two per arm. The blind adjudicator credited the same cross-view identity omission in Q11 for checklist, free-form, and perspective-guided review (2/2 in each arm). The preregistered incremental-rescue condition did not pass. Preserve this as a scoped null, not a claim that the methods are equivalent in general.
- **Relation to prior work:** #8088's A03 was a scoped null for free-form decomposition on a narrower packet. #8574 tested a different mechanism and contract stratum. Neither establishes human-review performance, omission prevalence, GUI safety, product benefit, or live computer-control correctness.
- **Next test:** No rerun of T0 A01. Reopen this line only through a distinct preregistered question with a justified broader/validated contract population or a human-review transfer design, independent adjudication, and a result that would change adoption. Any runtime or live-GUI claim requires separate evidence.
- **Limits:** The 16 authored contracts and same-family reviewers do not estimate real-world prevalence or human performance. This scoped null does not establish equivalence.

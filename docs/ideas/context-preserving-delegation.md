# Context-preserving delegation after successful local execution

- **Status:** The synthetic T0 passed within its stipulated model; caller-visible benefit in an actual GUI remains unverified.
- **Source:** Issue [#6373](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/issues/6373); merged evidence PR [#6433](https://github.com/Unjuno/agent-interface/pull/6433).
- **Idea:** After a delegated task succeeds, preserve its intended effects while restoring only declared caller context when still safe, cleaning temporary artifacts, and reporting residual differences. This is not snapshot rollback.
- **Outcome:** The one-shot synthetic candidate compared eight histories across four policies (32 rows). `RESTORE_PLUS_DIFF` had 5/8 fixed next-task fixtures with zero modeled context mismatches versus `SUMMARY_ONLY` at 3/8 (4 versus 9 total mismatches). Blind `RESTORE_ONLY` had 6/8 but produced four destructive, stale, or false-safe cases. The independent audit-only successor reconstructed the frozen rows without rerunning the candidate. `PASS_METHOD_SCOPED` applies only to this synthetic proxy.
- **Evidence:** Candidate, frozen inputs, raw, oracle, audit, and checksums are retained under [`context_preserving_delegation_6373_t0_v1`](../../research/analysis/context_preserving_delegation_6373_t0_v1/); the no-rerun audit successor is under [`context_preserving_delegation_6373_t0_a02_audit_v1`](../../research/analysis/context_preserving_delegation_6373_t0_a02_audit_v1/). The full result and limits are also indexed in [`IDEAS_AND_OUTCOMES.md`](../IDEAS_AND_OUTCOMES.md).
- **Does not establish:** GUI or human benefit, application callbacks, real latency, universal rollback, or safe restoration under concurrent external changes.
- **Distinct from:** #16's compensation after partial effects, #6102's nested modal matching, and #9004's bounded continuation during delayed supervision. These may provide mechanisms but do not answer post-success caller-context restoration.
- **Next discriminator:** On a disposable, supported GUI fixture, compare declared-context `RESTORE_PLUS_DIFF` with `SUMMARY_ONLY` plus a fresh observation. Pair the same chained tasks and preserve one intended persistent effect; include an externally changed context that must not be overwritten. Independently score the persistent effect and returned context, and record downstream task success, extra observations/actions, elapsed time, incorrect restoration, and residual differences. Stop if the context oracle cannot distinguish caller-owned state from external mutation. Preregister the exact fixture, oracle, and source before any fresh allocation; the 2026-10-02 candidate is consumed and must not be rerun.
- **Issue disposition:** Keep #6373 open/actionable for this untested GUI-level discriminator. The scoped T0 result is complete; the broader claim is not. No current owner or allocation is established by this catalog entry.
